## What it is

SiteGPT is a no-code AI chatbot for customer support. You give it your website URL or sitemap, upload files, paste text, or connect sources like Notion, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, GitHub, and YouTube. It trains a chatbot on that content, and you embed the chatbot on your site with a snippet or a WordPress plugin. The chatbot answers visitor questions from your own content, collects leads, and hands the chat to a person when needed.

It was built by indie founder Bhanu Teja, who launched it in April 2023. He later sold his other SaaS product, Feather, to focus on SiteGPT. SiteGPT now runs on OpenAI's GPT-4.1 and GPT-4.1 mini models. It also answers on WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and email, and it connects to help desks like Zendesk, Crisp, and Freshdesk Messaging.

## Who it's for

- Small businesses and online stores that get the same questions over and over and want answers around the clock.
- SaaS founders who want a bot trained on their docs and help center, with a handoff to a person for the hard cases.
- Teams already on Zendesk, Crisp, Slack, or Freshdesk Messaging who want an AI first reply without changing help desks.
- Agencies that want to resell chatbots to clients. SiteGPT runs a separate white-label agency program with a $500/month minimum.

It's not a good fit if you need complex chat flows, deep CRM automation, or a full ticketing inbox. It's also a poor fit if your traffic is high but your budget is small, because every plan has a monthly message cap.

## Standout details

- **Trains from many sources.** Websites, sitemaps, files (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, CSV, and more), YouTube videos, raw text, and connected apps. Per the docs, uploads now go up to 1 GB, and scanned PDFs get text recognition.
- **Human handoff on every plan.** Visitors can ask for a person, and your team takes over in Chat History. Working hours, escalation rules, and a choice between AI and a person were added in September and October 2026. An iPhone app (Growth and up) sends a push alert when a visitor asks for a human.
- **More than a website widget.** WhatsApp and AI email support are paid add-ons. In October 2026, the email add-on started writing draft replies for your team. Messenger, Google Chat, Slack, Crisp, Freshdesk Messaging, and Zoho SalesIQ connect on Growth and above, and Zendesk Messaging connects on Scale.
- **Built for developers and AI agents too.** An API, a CLI, SDKs, and an MCP server (added May 2026) let you build and manage chatbots from code or from AI assistants. These start on the Growth plan.
- **Shows what visitors ask.** Growth adds daily trends, an unanswered-questions list with one-click fixes, CSV export, and a weekly email digest. Scale adds AI topic, sentiment, and outcome tagging.
- **Security paperwork for a small vendor.** SiteGPT says it passed a SOC 2 Type II audit and was assessed for GDPR and HIPAA. It says your data is never used to train AI models. A signed BAA for HIPAA is available on Enterprise.
- **Ships monthly.** The public changelog lists monthly releases going back to March 2023, including a long November 2026 list.

## Pricing

SiteGPT is paid. Every self-serve plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and you need a card to start it. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. You can ask to extend the trial by 7 days from the billing page. There is no free plan. SiteGPT's billing docs say it gives **no refunds after a payment, including on yearly plans.** Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing) as of November 19, 2026, and don't include tax.

- **Starter: $59/month, or $39/month billed yearly ($468/year).** 1 chatbot, 1 member, 1,000 pages of content, and 4,000 GPT-4.1 mini messages (or 400 GPT-4.1 messages) a month. Manual refresh and basic analytics only. No chat integrations, API, or iPhone app.
- **Growth: $129/month, or $79/month billed yearly ($948/year).** 2 chatbots, 4 members, 10,000 pages, and 10,000 mini messages (or 1,000 GPT-4.1). Adds chat integrations, the API, CLI, and MCP, the iPhone app, monthly auto-refresh, and advanced analytics.
- **Scale: $429/month, or $259/month billed yearly ($3,108/year).** 3 chatbots, 10 members, 50,000 pages, and 40,000 mini messages (or 4,000 GPT-4.1). Adds Zendesk Messaging, webhooks, weekly auto-refresh, daily sitemap scans, and AI conversation insights.
- **Enterprise: custom pricing.** Up to 1,000 chatbots, custom message volume, daily refresh, priority support, a DPA, and a BAA for HIPAA.

Add-ons cost $59/month each, or $39/month billed yearly. They are removing the "Powered by SiteGPT" line, extra 5,000-message packs, WhatsApp (per number), and AI email support (per inbox).

## Honest verdict

SiteGPT does the core job well. Point it at your site, and within minutes you have a chatbot that answers from your own content. It can collect leads and hand off to a person. Reviewers on G2, Capterra, and Product Hunt mostly praise the quick setup and the accurate answers. It's also one of the more actively built indie tools in this space, with monthly releases and a SOC 2 Type II audit behind it. The catch is cost. The $39 Starter plan leaves out integrations, the API, and advanced analytics. Message caps apply on every plan, and branding removal, extra messages, WhatsApp, and email support are paid add-ons. There are no refunds after you pay, so use the 7-day trial on your real content and real traffic before it bills you.

### Pros

- Very fast setup. Reviewers say a working chatbot takes minutes: enter a URL and it trains.
- Answers come from your own content. Recent updates made answers cite the page they used.
- Human handoff and lead capture are on every plan, not locked behind a top tier.
- Reaches beyond the website widget to WhatsApp, Messenger, email, Slack, and Zendesk.
- An API, CLI, SDKs, and MCP server for developers and AI-agent workflows.
- A SOC 2 Type II audit, GDPR and HIPAA assessments, and a stated policy of never training on your data.
- An indie founder who ships monthly, with a detailed public changelog.

### Cons

- Starter is thin: 1 chatbot, 1 seat, manual refresh, basic analytics, and no integrations or API. Most businesses will want Growth at $79 to $129/month.
- Message caps apply on every plan, and extra packs cost $39 to $59/month per 5,000 messages. Busy sites can outgrow a plan quickly.
- Add-ons pile up. Removing "Powered by SiteGPT," WhatsApp, and AI email support each cost extra.
- No refunds after a payment, including on yearly plans.
- G2 and Capterra reviewers mention limited widget customization, few deep integrations (one asked for Zapier and HubSpot), and replies that can feel rigid. A Reddit commenter said it lacks workflow logic for complex flows.
- A few Product Hunt reviewers reported billing and account problems, including a cancel button that didn't work. The review base is small, with about a dozen reviews each on G2 and Product Hunt.
- Scale costs $259/month billed yearly and still covers only 3 chatbots.
