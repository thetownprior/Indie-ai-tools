## What it is

HeadshotPro is an AI headshot generator. You upload a few selfies from your phone or laptop, pick backdrops and outfits from a large style library (or tap "Choose for me"), and it generates a set of 30 to 70 professional headshots. Depending on the package, the set is ready in about 2 hours, 30 minutes, or 15 minutes. You download single JPEGs or the whole set as a ZIP.

After the set is done, you can edit it. Photo credits let you swap a backdrop, change an outfit, remove a blemish, or add headroom. A built-in profile picture editor crops, shapes, and brands a headshot for LinkedIn, Slack, email signatures, or print. Photos that fail HeadshotPro's upload standards get flagged before generation, so a bad selfie doesn't waste the shoot.

There is also a team product. An admin buys credits, picks the styles everyone can use, and invites people by email or link. Members upload their own selfies, and the admin tracks who has finished and downloads everything in bulk.

HeadshotPro was founded by indie developer Danny Postma, who earlier built and sold the AI copywriting tool Headlime. It launched in 2023 with no outside funding. The about page says it has passed 17 million headshots, 200,000 shoots, and $10 million in revenue. LinkedIn lists a team of four.

## Who it's for

HeadshotPro fits anyone who needs a current, professional photo and doesn't want to book a studio: freelancers, consultants, coaches, job seekers, real estate agents, and founders updating LinkedIn, a CV, a speaker bio, or an About page. If you post on LinkedIn a lot, a fresh headshot pairs naturally with a tool like [Supergrow](/tools/supergrow/).

The team plan suits small businesses and agencies that want matching photos on a team page, including for new hires who join later.

It is not for people who want casual or lifestyle shots as the main output, or for anyone who needs a photo that is guaranteed to be a real, unaltered image of them. Every result is AI-generated.

## Standout details

- **One-time price, no subscription.** Individuals pay once per shoot. Team credits never expire, so a company can buy a batch and use it as people join.
- **30-day money-back guarantee.** The refund policy and Help Center say you can get a full refund within 30 days of paying, for any reason, through a self-serve refund page. A redo does not affect that right.
- **Editing after generation.** Photo credits cover backdrop swaps, outfit changes, and blemish removal, and Professional and Executive include one free redo within 60 days.
- **Team tools.** Admins curate a shared style pool, send invites and automatic reminders, track progress, and bulk download. Team plans add a REST API, webhooks, and Zapier, and SSO is a paid add-on.
- **Free taster.** A free headshot generator on the site takes one selfie with no sign-up and lets you download one HD result.
- **Stated privacy terms.** HeadshotPro says your photos are never used to train AI models, and input photos are deleted 30 days after generation. It also lists a SOC 2 Type II report and a DPA through its Trust Center.

## Pricing

HeadshotPro is paid, with one-time prices. Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://www.headshotpro.com/pricing) as of November 20, 2026.

- **Basic: $29 one-time.** 30 headshots in about 2 hours, with 6 photo credits for edits.
- **Professional: $39 one-time.** 50 headshots in about 30 minutes, 26 photo credits, and 1 free redo within 60 days.
- **Executive: $59 one-time.** 70 headshots in about 15 minutes, 46 photo credits, 4K resolution, portrait and landscape formats, and the free redo.
- **Team: from $19.50 per person.** One credit is one person's full shoot of 40 headshots with one free retry. Volume discounts stack across lifetime purchases, from 20% off at 5 credits to 50% off at 1,000 or more. Pay by card, PayPal, bank transfer, or invoice.
- **Enterprise: custom pricing** for 1,000+ people, with SSO and SCIM, a signed MSA, and net-30 invoicing.

Individual purchases are covered by the 30-day money-back guarantee. Team members can't request refunds themselves; the team lead contacts support.

## Honest verdict

HeadshotPro is one of the most-recommended AI headshot generators, and the reasons hold up. It's fast, the price is a one-time payment, and Reddit testers and Trustpilot reviewers often call its results among the most realistic they've tried. The catch is the keeper rate. HeadshotPro's own Help Center says most photos in a set are not keepers, and buyers report a handful of good shots mixed in with duds and faces that don't look like them. Results depend heavily on the selfies you upload. Follow the photo guidelines, pick your keepers inside the 30-day refund window, and treat it as a cheap alternative to a studio, not a replacement for a good photographer.

### Pros

- One-time pricing from $29, with no subscription and team credits that never expire.
- Fast turnaround: about 2 hours on Basic down to about 15 minutes on Executive.
- Reviewers on Reddit and Trustpilot often rank its realism near the top of AI headshot tools.
- A full refund within 30 days of payment, for any reason, through a self-serve page.
- Post-generation editing (backdrops, outfits, blemishes) and a free redo on the $39 and $59 packages.
- A real team workflow with shared styles, reminders, bulk download, API, and Zapier.
- A bootstrapped indie company that has been shipping since 2023 in a crowded category.

### Cons

- Most photos in a set won't be usable. HeadshotPro says so itself, and buyers typically report a handful of keepers, sometimes with odd skin marks or faces that don't look like them.
- Quality depends heavily on your input selfies. Poor lighting or few angles give weaker results.
- The site's refund messaging is inconsistent. The refund policy says 30 days, other pages say "no expiry," and the affiliate page says 14 days. Older Trustpilot reviews describe refunds denied after downloading a photo. Go by the 30-day policy and request any refund early.
- Basic ($29) has no free redo and only 6 edit credits, so most people will want the $39 package.
- The free headshot tool is limited. One Trustpilot reviewer called the free result an untouched photo with the background removed.
- Packages hold fewer headshots than they did in 2024 at the same prices, per third-party reviews and older pricing write-ups.
- It's built for professional looks only. One Reddit tester wished for casual photos.
- General AI image tools now compete directly, and the founder has reportedly said that cut HeadshotPro's revenue projections.
