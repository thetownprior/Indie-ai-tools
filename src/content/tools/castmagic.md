## What it is

Castmagic is an AI content tool for long recordings. You paste a link (YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, TikTok, Reels), upload a file, connect Zoom, Loom, or Google Drive, or point it at an RSS feed. It turns each recording into a speaker-labelled, timestamped transcript in 60+ languages. From there it drafts show notes, summaries, newsletters, blog posts, LinkedIn and X posts, quotes, and more. Castmagic says it has more than 100 of these outputs.

It has grown past one-off drafts. A content pipeline runs a set of drafts on every new recording automatically. Studio cuts captioned vertical clips, audiograms, and image carousels. A content strategist answers questions across your whole library and suggests what to publish next. Asks sends guests or customers a link to answer questions in their own time. Audience Chat is a chatbot for your own site that answers from your episodes. There is also a REST API and a connector that lets Claude search your Castmagic library.

Castmagic was started in 2023 by Ramon Berrios and Blaine Bolus, hosts of the DTC Pod podcast, with engineer Justin Tormey. They built it to handle their own show's content. Berrios has written on Indie Hackers that the company bootstrapped to $1M ARR with no outside capital. A Refresh Miami profile put the team at around ten people.

## Who it's for

Castmagic fits anyone who records something long every week and wants it turned into written and social content: podcast hosts, YouTubers, coaches, course creators, and agencies or small marketing teams handling several shows or clients. Hobby suits a solo show. Starter adds API access and more hours for a growing creator. Team is for agencies and content teams.

It is a poor fit if you only need transcripts or only need short clips. Cheaper single-purpose tools do those jobs, and Castmagic's price only makes sense if you use the writing side too.

## Standout details

- **Import from almost anywhere.** Links, file uploads, Zoom, Loom, Google Drive, Zapier, the iOS app, and RSS feeds. Autopilot pulls in each new episode from a feed when it is published.
- **Drafts on autopilot.** Recurring prompts run your chosen set of drafts (for example show notes, a newsletter, and social posts) on every recording as it lands, shaped by a brand voice and your own custom prompts.
- **Transcripts you can export.** Speaker labels, timestamps, and one-click translation, with exports to SRT, VTT, PDF, and DOCX.
- **Studio for visual formats.** Captioned clips in 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9, audiograms, and AI-drafted image carousels. Clipping and audiograms are unlimited on every plan.
- **Ask your whole back catalog.** The content strategist and Magic Chat answer across many recordings and cite the moments they draw from. The Claude connector gives Claude read-only access to your library.
- **A chatbot for your site.** Audience Chat is installed with one script tag and answers visitor questions from your episodes, linking to the timestamp. It declines questions your library doesn't cover and can collect email addresses as leads.

## Pricing

Castmagic is paid only. There is no free plan on the [pricing page](https://www.castmagic.io/pricing) as of November 14, 2026. Castmagic's contact page FAQ says new users get a free trial to try a few episodes, but the pricing page does not list one, so check at sign-up. Annual billing saves about 30%.

- **Hobby: $29/month, or $19/month billed yearly ($239/year).** 30 transcribed hours, up to 3 spaces, 50GB storage, up to 3 seats, unlimited AI outputs, re-generations, clips, and audiograms. No API access.
- **Starter: $69/month, or $48/month billed yearly ($579/year).** 100 transcribed hours, up to 10 spaces, 250GB storage, 5 seats, API access, and an onboarding call.
- **Team: $199/month, or $139/month billed yearly ($1,669/year).** 400 transcribed hours, up to 25 spaces, 1TB storage, 5 seats included (extra seats $19/month or $159/year each), and priority support.
- **Business & Scale: from $699/month.** Custom volume, seats, and storage, with an SLA and dedicated support. Booked through a demo.

Transcribed hours are a library total, not a monthly allowance. You can buy more hours anytime, but the pricing page doesn't list the price. Castmagic's terms of service say payments are non-refundable unless stated otherwise, and partial months are not refunded. If you cancel, your plan runs to the end of the billing period.

## Honest verdict

Castmagic does more with one recording than most repurposing tools, and long-time podcasters on Reddit and Capterra say it has become a fixed part of their show-notes workflow. The trade-offs are price and polish. Several users call it expensive for independent podcasters, the AI drafts often need editing to sound like you, and the no-refund terms with no free plan mean you are paying to find out whether it suits you.

### Pros

- One upload gives you a transcript, show notes, newsletter, blog, social posts, clips, and carousels, and the content pipeline can draft them all automatically.
- Users praise it most for episode notes, timestamps, and titles. One r/podcasting user who has used it for 2–3 years calls it the best of the similar tools.
- Searching across your whole library, a site chatbot, the API, and the Claude connector make an archive of episodes useful again.
- Clips, audiograms, and AI re-generations are unlimited on every plan, so the hour limit is the main thing to watch.
- A bootstrapped team led by working podcasters. Capterra reviewers mention frequent updates and access to the founders through the Slack community.

### Cons

- No free plan, and the terms say payments are non-refundable. Start on monthly billing rather than a year upfront.
- Expensive for hobbyists. "Comprehensive but expensive" and "sort of expensive" are typical comments on r/podcasting.
- AI drafts can read as generic. Some Reddit users say they rewrite most of the text or get better results from a tuned general chatbot.
- Hours are a library total (30 on Hobby), so a weekly show with a back catalog can use them up quickly, and the price of extra hours isn't published.
- AppSumo reviewers have reported clumsy transcript editing, slow or failed YouTube imports, and weak support for non-English and right-to-left languages. Test your own content in the trial or first month.
