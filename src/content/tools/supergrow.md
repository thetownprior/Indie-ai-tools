## What it is

Supergrow is an AI content platform built only for LinkedIn. It helps you come up with post ideas, write drafts that try to match your voice, turn them into carousels, and schedule them. Its main hook is PostCast: an AI interviewer called Alex asks you questions about your expertise, and Supergrow says each 15-minute session becomes 3 to 5 LinkedIn drafts.

Around that sit a voice profile it calls Content DNA, repurposing from YouTube videos, articles, and PDFs, a calendar and Kanban board for drafts, auto first comments, an Engage area for commenting on other people's posts, and profile analytics. Supergrow says it is a LinkedIn Verified Partner and publishes through LinkedIn's official APIs, with no scraping or browser automation, and it never asks for your LinkedIn password.

Supergrow was co-founded by Deven Bhooshan (CEO) and Utsav Patel (CMO). Its about page lists a small team spread across India.

## Who it's for

Supergrow fits people for whom LinkedIn is the main channel: founders, consultants, coaches, and executives who know their subject but don't enjoy writing, and would rather talk through an idea than face a blank page. It also targets LinkedIn ghostwriters and agencies, who get a separate workspace and Content DNA per client, and marketing teams running employee advocacy for up to five people on the Teams plan.

It is a poor fit if you also post on X or other networks, want deep analytics, or need lead lists and outreach. If you want one tool for X and LinkedIn, see [Typefully](/tools/typefully/).

## Standout details

- **PostCast AI interviews.** You talk, and an AI interviewer adapts its follow-up questions. Supergrow says one 15-minute session gives 3 to 5 drafts. Unlimited PostCast sessions are included on Pro.
- **Content DNA voice profile.** It learns your tone, vocabulary, and topics from your past LinkedIn posts. Since October 2026 you can also build it by pasting at least 1,000 characters describing how you write, or writing samples, if you have no post history.
- **Content Radar (beta).** Connect Granola and Slack, and it pulls post ideas from your meetings and threads and checks which ones your market is discussing. It opened to every workspace on November 10, 2026.
- **Repurposing and carousels.** Turn YouTube videos, blog posts, and PDFs into LinkedIn posts, and turn a post into branded carousel slides.
- **Scheduling built for LinkedIn.** Calendar view, recurring time slots, a Kanban board from draft to scheduled, and an auto first comment when a post goes live.
- **Review links with no login.** Ghostwriters and teams can send a post for review. Reviewers comment and approve from an emailed link without an account (added November 12, 2026).
- **Works from Claude or ChatGPT.** The Supergrow MCP lets you draft and schedule posts and pull analytics from an AI assistant. It is included on Pro.
- **Ships often.** The public changelog shows dated releases every couple of weeks, most recently two-factor sign-in on November 18, 2026.

## Pricing

Supergrow is paid, with a 7-day free trial on both paid plans. Per its pricing page, if you don't pick a plan when the trial ends, your account pauses instead of charging you. There is no free plan. Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing) as of November 18, 2026.

- **Pro: $39/month, or $31/month billed yearly.** One LinkedIn account, with Content DNA, unlimited PostCast interviews, repurposing, scheduling, Kanban, auto first comment, carousel maker, Engage, the MCP, profile analytics, and a weekly report.
- **Teams: $199/month, or $189/month billed yearly.** Five accounts, plus approval workflows, an organization dashboard, a shared calendar, publishing on behalf of members, team analytics, challenges and a leaderboard, and Slack notifications.
- **Enterprise: custom pricing.** For more than five accounts, with API access, an SLA, custom onboarding, and invoice billing.

You can upgrade or downgrade any time. Upgrades are prorated, and downgrades take effect at the next billing date. Older reviews list a $19 Starter plan; it is no longer on the pricing page.

## Honest verdict

Supergrow is a well-built LinkedIn workflow: talk into PostCast, get drafts in roughly your voice, and ship them on a schedule through LinkedIn's official API. Reviewers widely praise PostCast, the clean interface, and the safe publishing setup. They also agree on the limits: analytics are light, it covers LinkedIn only, and the AI drafts can drift into generic LinkedIn style, so you still need to edit. Use the 7-day trial to see whether its drafts sound like you before you pay.

### Pros

- PostCast is a genuinely different way to make content. Talking through ideas suits people who won't write.
- Publishes through LinkedIn's official API, so there's no browser extension or password sharing and less account risk.
- Pro includes everything an individual needs: unlimited interviews, carousels, scheduling, Engage, analytics, and the MCP.
- Strong workflow for ghostwriters and teams: per-client voice profiles and approval links that don't need a login.
- The trial pauses instead of charging you, and the product ships updates every couple of weeks.

### Cons

- LinkedIn only. It does nothing for X, Instagram, or a newsletter.
- Drafts still need editing. Several reviewers and Reddit users say the output can sound polished but generic after a while, and one tester said corrections don't carry over between AI chat sessions.
- Analytics are light. Reviewers say they go little beyond what LinkedIn shows, and per-post scores compare posts only with your own average.
- No cheap entry point anymore. The lowest price is $31/month billed yearly, and going from one account to Teams jumps to $189 to $199/month for five seats, even if you need only two.
- The review base is small. Product Hunt shows one review, and independent reviewers counted only about 13 to 14 reviews behind its G2 score.
- No lead database, messaging, or outreach tools, unlike broader LinkedIn growth platforms.
