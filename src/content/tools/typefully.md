## What it is

Typefully is a writing and scheduling app for social posts. You draft in a focused editor, see a preview of exactly how a post or thread will look, and publish or schedule it to X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, and Substack Notes, one at a time or all at once.

It started in 2020 as a side project by Fabrizio Rinaldi for writing Twitter threads, built with his co-founder Francesco Di Lorenzo (the pair also made Mailbrew). It is still a small team. Its jobs page describes the company as "small & mighty," remote, and async. Twitter co-founder Ev Williams invested in 2022, according to Typefully's own announcement.

The AI part is a built-in Writing Assistant. It learns your tone from your writing samples and drafts, turns rough notes or voice-note transcripts into posts, rewrites drafts, adapts a post for another platform, and writes image alt text.

## Who it's for

Typefully suits people whose social presence is mostly writing: indie founders building in public, newsletter writers, consultants, and small marketing teams posting to X and LinkedIn. The Business plan adds shared drafts, comments, roles, and Slack notifications, so it works for agencies and startups where several people review posts before they go out.

It is a weaker fit if you post mostly images or video to Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube. Those networks are not on its platform list.

## Standout details

- **Preview what you'll get.** The editor shows threads, mentions, and media as they will look on each platform before you publish.
- **Write once, post everywhere.** Cross-post one draft to X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, and Substack. Polls carry over to LinkedIn, Threads, and Mastodon as well as X (added October 2026).
- **AI that works from your own writing.** The Writing Assistant learns from your samples. Since October 2026 it can also work through a teammate's comments on a draft, revise the posts they point at, and resolve them, with an undo.
- **Built for agents and automation.** A public API, an MCP server, and "AI Agent Skills" are included even on the free plan, along with integrations for Claude, ChatGPT, Notion, Raycast, Zapier, and Slack.
- **Growth tools for X.** Auto-retweets, auto-plugs (a follow-up post once a tweet hits a like or retweet threshold), auto-DM campaigns, and a thread finisher.
- **LinkedIn extras.** PDF carousels and a scheduled first comment.
- **Ships often.** The public changelog had four updates in October 2026 alone, including an easier Bluesky sign-in that supports custom PDS servers.

## Pricing

Typefully is freemium. Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://typefully.com/pricing) as of November 11, 2026.

- **Free: $0.** 10 posts a month, 1 social set, 1 user, and limited media uploads. Scheduling, the calendar, every platform, shareable drafts, the API, and MCP are included. No AI writing features and no X analytics.
- **Creator: $99/year.** Annual billing only. 1,000 posts a month, the AI writing features, X analytics (detailed metrics, profile conversion rate, streaks, CSV export), and unlimited media uploads. Still 1 social set and 1 user.
- **Business: $18/month per social set, billed yearly.** 1,500 posts a month, up to 50 social sets, unlimited users, teams, roles and permissions, comments with Slack notifications, higher API rate limits, and more included AI usage.

## Honest verdict

Typefully is one of the most pleasant ways to write and schedule text posts, and the Creator plan is cheap for what it does. Reviewers on Product Hunt and Reddit keep praising the clean, distraction-free editor and how easy it is to schedule threads. The catches are the small free tier, the annual-only Creator plan, and an AI assistant that works best when you give it real ideas instead of asking it to come up with them.

### Pros

- Clean, fast editor with accurate previews. It's the reason most users stay.
- Publishes to six platforms, including Bluesky, Mastodon, and Substack Notes, which many schedulers skip.
- Creator works out to about $8.25 a month for 1,000 posts plus AI and analytics.
- API, MCP, and agent integrations come with every plan, including free.
- Small, independent team that ships updates every month.

### Cons

- The free plan allows only 10 posts a month and has no AI features, so it's really a trial.
- Creator is billed yearly only, with no monthly option to test it for a few weeks.
- Analytics cover X only. LinkedIn and the other platforms get publishing, not detailed metrics.
- No Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or Facebook, so visual-first creators will need another tool.
- Product Hunt reviewers mention occasional collaboration glitches, attachment size limits, and a "best time to post" feature that is hit-or-miss.
- As with any AI writer, posts can come out sounding generic. Reddit users testing X tools, Typefully included, say AI-assisted posts tend to sound alike unless you edit them heavily.
