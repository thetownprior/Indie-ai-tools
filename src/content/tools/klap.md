## What it is

Klap is an AI video clipping tool. You paste a link or upload a long video, and it picks the most interesting moments and cuts them into vertical clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Each clip gets active-speaker reframing, animated captions, a virality score, and an AI-written post caption. You can then edit clips in Klap and schedule them to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook.

It works on any video where people talk: podcasts, interviews, webinars, tutorials, product reviews, and gaming or sports commentary. Klap says it transcribes and edits in 99+ languages. Sources can be YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo, Loom, Google Drive, and Dropbox links, direct video links, or file uploads, up to 3 hours per video.

Klap is run by ZIGG SAS, a company incorporated in France. The site footer credits two makers, Theo and Victor.

## Who it's for

Klap fits creators who already record long content and want a steady stream of shorts without editing each one by hand: podcasters, YouTubers, streamers, coaches, and course creators. It also targets "clippers" who post clips at volume for view-based campaigns, since its plans count clips instead of source minutes.

It suits people who will review clips before posting. If you need every short to be post-ready with no fixes, or your videos have little speech, look elsewhere or test carefully first (see Cons).

## Standout details

- **Priced by clips, not minutes.** Plans include 100, 300, or 1,000 clips a month. A long source video does not use up your plan, only the clips you make do. Klap puts the cost at $0.29 a clip on Basic, $0.26 on Pro, and $0.19 on Pro+.
- **You can steer the AI.** Let it pick moments, describe a moment in your own words, or set the start and end yourself. You also choose how many clips you want from each video.
- **AI Reframe 2.** The newest reframing analyzes scenes and applies layouts such as Split Screen, Screencast, Gaming, and Bubble.
- **Publishing built in.** Schedule to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook, with unlimited social accounts and post analytics on every plan.
- **Works from AI assistants.** A ChatGPT app, a Claude connector, and an MCP server with 18 tools let an assistant clip, caption, export, and schedule from a prompt, signed in with your Klap account.
- **Developer API.** A self-serve API is priced separately at $0.44 per input video, $0.32 per short, and $0.48 per export, with Zapier, Make, and n8n integrations.

## Pricing

Klap is paid. You can try it free on 1 video with no credit card: Klap turns that video into clips you can preview and edit, but downloading and posting need a paid plan. Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://klap.app/pricing), which Klap says was last updated November 4, 2026. Yearly billing takes 50% off and is charged as one payment up front.

- **Basic: $29/month, or $174/year ($14.50/month).** 100 clips a month.
- **Pro: $79/month, or $474/year ($39.50/month).** 300 clips a month.
- **Pro+: $189/month, or $1,134/year ($94.50/month).** 1,000 clips a month, aimed at teams.

Every plan includes AI clipping, unlimited social accounts, analytics, and exports with no watermark. Yearly plans get the whole year's clips at once. Per Klap's billing help page, you can get a full refund within your first month on monthly and yearly plans, and refunds for up to your 3 most recent unused months after that. You must cancel first, from your billing page, then ask for the refund in live chat or by email.

## Honest verdict

Klap does the core job well for speech-led video: drop in a long recording and get a batch of captioned, reframed shorts in minutes, at a clear price per clip. Reviewers on Reddit, Unite.AI, and DEV Community agree it saves real time, and also agree its output is a first draft, not a finished post. The bigger risk is the business side. Low-star Trustpilot reviews repeatedly describe surprise charges, a hard-to-find cancel option, and slow support, and a Product Hunt reviewer reported plan changes after they had paid.

### Pros

- Fast. Paste a link and get many captioned vertical clips from one long video.
- Clip-based plans make costs predictable, especially for long podcasts and streams.
- You can direct which moments get cut instead of relying only on the AI's picks.
- Scheduling to five platforms and unlimited social accounts are included on every plan.
- No watermark on paid exports, and a free trial video with no credit card.
- Unusually broad automation: API, MCP server, ChatGPT and Claude apps, Zapier, Make, and n8n.

### Cons

- Clips need review. Independent reviewers report clips that cut sentences early, miss the best moments, or need caption and framing fixes, and one Reddit user called their results unusable.
- Billing complaints are common. Low-star Trustpilot reviews describe charges after a cancel request, an unexpected yearly charge on upgrade, and trouble finding how to cancel. Klap replies publicly and has offered refunds in some cases. Check which billing period you are choosing at checkout.
- Support gets poor marks from some users, who describe unanswered emails, chat, and Discord messages.
- The free trial is a preview. You can't download or post anything without paying.
- Clips made with default styles can look like everyone else's, so plan to add your own hooks and branding.
