## What it is

Fliki is a browser-based AI video and voice tool. You start from an idea, a script, a blog post URL, or a PowerPoint file, and Fliki writes or splits the script into scenes, adds an AI voiceover, picks stock or AI-generated visuals and music, and opens the result in an editor where you can change any scene. It can also produce audio on its own (voiceovers and audiobooks), plus thumbnails, social carousels, and presentations.

It is made by a small team led by co-founders Atul Yadav and Sabir Ahmed, under the company Nine Thirty Five LLC. The product started life as Awedio, a text-to-audio tool, before it was renamed Fliki and added video. The changelog is busy: in October and November 2026 alone the team shipped a product-link-to-video maker, a video podcast maker, Swap Studio (swap faces, outfits, or backgrounds in existing footage), and an AI Director that drafts a script and storyboard in chat and shows the exact credit cost before you generate.

## Who it's for

Fliki suits people who need a steady stream of narrated videos but do not want to film or record themselves: faceless YouTube and social channels, course creators and trainers, marketers making explainers and product videos, and small businesses that want the same video in several languages. It is less suited to editors who want frame-level control, since Reddit and G2 reviewers note that editing is more limited than in a traditional video editor and that long videos still need human polish.

## Standout details

- **Many ways in.** Idea to video, script to video, blog post to video, PPT to video, product link to video, and recording to polished video, all ending in the same scene-based editor.
- **A very large voice library.** The free plan has 300 voices across 80+ languages. Standard has 1,000 voices (500 of them ultra-realistic), and Premium has 2,000+, including multilingual expressive voices.
- **Voice cloning on paid plans.** Standard allows 1 cloned voice and Premium allows 3.
- **Avatars and characters.** Standard includes limited stock avatars and custom characters. Premium adds all AI avatars, photo avatars, and AI video clips.
- **Clear credit rules.** Stock and uploaded media cost 0 credits, and replaying a scene without editing its text is free. AI voices cost 0.25 to 2 credits per minute depending on the voice, and AI video clips cost 0.1 to 6 credits per second depending on the model.
- **Translate and publish.** Paid plans can translate videos into 80+ languages and publish to YouTube, with a monthly publishing allowance (50 on Standard, 120 on Premium).
- **Automation.** Make and Zapier integrations and bulk create are listed for paid plans, and Premium includes API access.

## Pricing

Fliki is freemium. Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://fliki.ai/pricing) as of November 15, 2026, in USD per user per month. Yearly billing takes 25% off.

- **Free: $0.** 3 credits per month, 300 voices, videos up to 5 minutes, 720p exports, and a Fliki watermark. Most workflows are available in limited form. No credit card needed.
- **Standard: $28/month, or $21/month billed yearly ($252/year).** 180 credits per month, 1,000 voices with 500 ultra-realistic, 1080p exports, videos up to 15 minutes, translation into 80+ languages, 1 voice clone, limited stock avatars, custom characters and series, and commercial rights.
- **Premium: $88/month, or $66/month billed yearly ($792/year).** 600 credits per month, 2,000+ voices, videos up to 40 minutes, AI video clips and photo avatars, all AI avatars, 3 voice clones, multiple brand kits, custom fonts, priority support, and API access.
- **Enterprise: custom pricing, billed yearly.** Custom credits, invoiced billing, personalized avatars, professional voice cloning, and a dedicated account manager.

Monthly credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. Yearly plans get 12 months of credits as one yearly pool. Since April 2026, paid users can buy top-up credit packs, which last 180 days but need an active subscription to use.

Fliki's FAQ says paid subscriptions are not refunded once purchased, because of the AI costs behind each generation. Its terms say some refund requests may be considered case by case. You can cancel renewal at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.

## Honest verdict

Fliki is one of the quickest ways to go from text to a finished, narrated video, and its voice library and language coverage are its biggest strengths. G2 reviewers mostly praise how easy it is and how many languages it supports. The catches are money and support: AI-generated visuals eat credits fast, there are no refunds as a rule, and recent Reddit threads describe very slow support replies. Build a few real videos on the free plan, and start monthly before you pay for a year.

### Pros

- Very easy to start. Ease of use is the most common praise on G2 and Reddit.
- Huge voice library across 80+ languages, plus voice cloning on paid plans.
- Turns existing content (blog posts, slides, scripts, product pages) into video without filming.
- Stock media and replays cost no credits, and the AI Director shows the credit cost before you generate.
- Active development, with several major features shipped in October and November 2026.

### Cons

- Credits disappear quickly once you use AI video clips or avatars. At up to 6 credits per second for some AI video models, Standard's 180 monthly credits can cover well under a minute of that footage.
- The free plan is tight: 3 credits a month, 720p, and a watermark.
- No refunds on paid plans as a general rule, so a yearly plan is a real commitment.
- Support gets poor reports. In May 2026, Reddit users described live chat that showed agents online but did not answer, and replies that arrived weeks or months later by email.
- G2 reviewers mention some voices sounding robotic or mispronouncing unusual words, occasional mismatched stock media, and less editing control than a full video editor.
- Fliki's own help pages are not fully in sync with the pricing page (for example, older FAQ answers list different free and Premium limits), so check the pricing page before you buy.
