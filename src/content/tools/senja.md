## What it is

Senja is a testimonial and social proof tool. You share a collection form, and customers leave a text review or record a video in their browser. You can also import praise you already have from review sites and social platforms such as Google, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Product Hunt, X, and LinkedIn. Then you tag and approve it, and show it on your site with embeddable widgets, a hosted "Wall of Love" page, testimonial images, or social video clips.

The AI features sit on top of that library. Senja transcribes and tags video testimonials when they arrive, so you can search them by what was said. Testimonial analysis reads hundreds of testimonials and shows what customers say they like about you, in their own words. A case study generator turns one testimonial into a draft with a title, challenge, solution, results, and pull quote. Proof Reels picks sound bites from your video testimonials and edits them into a captioned reel for social posts or ads.

It began as a side project, per a 2022 Reddit launch post from its developer. Senja's about page now lists a small remote team of four, led by founder and CEO Oliver Meakings.

## Who it's for

Senja fits anyone whose sales depend on trust: course creators, coaches, newsletter writers, freelancers, agencies, and small SaaS companies that want proof on their landing pages, sales pages, and pitches. The free plan works for someone just starting to collect reviews. Starter suits a solo business with one brand. Pro is for teams and agencies running several projects.

It is less suited to local businesses that mainly need to manage Google reviews at scale, or to anyone who mostly wants star ratings next to their own company in Google search (see Cons).

## Standout details

- **Collect and import in one place.** Customers record video in the browser with no app or upload, and existing reviews come in from dozens of platforms. A Chrome extension lets you search your library while you work.
- **AI on your own testimonials.** Analysis, case study drafts, and Proof Reels all work from what customers actually said. Senja says the case study generator leaves an "[add detail]" gap instead of inventing numbers or quotes.
- **Free case study generator.** A standalone version on Senja's site works with no sign-up and no email.
- **Verified marks.** Testimonials can carry a "Verified purchase" mark (when the author paid you through your connected Stripe account) or a "Verified" mark (imported by Senja from the original post and not edited since), each linked to a public verification page.
- **More than collection.** Spin the Wheel rewards customers for leaving a testimonial, and you can send thank-you notes, e-gifts, coupons, and feature unlocks from the app.
- **No lock-in.** Every plan, including free, can export testimonials. Video exports are SD on Free and HD on paid plans.
- **Unlimited widgets on every plan.** Free accounts get unlimited widgets and Walls of Love with more than 20 templates, plus widget view and click analytics.

## Pricing

Senja is freemium. Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://senja.io/pricing) as of November 13, 2026. Yearly billing gets you 2 months free.

- **Free: $0.** Up to 15 text and video testimonials, a collection form, invites, imports, testimonial analysis, case study generation, Reels, unlimited widgets and Walls of Love, and exports. Senja branding is shown. Once you pass 15, new testimonials are hidden until you upgrade, though forms and widgets keep working.
- **Starter: $29/month.** Unlimited testimonials, 3 forms, 1 project, 2 seats, your own branding, custom domains, HD video exports, auto-sync from other sources, Spin the Wheel, Zapier, and the API and webhooks.
- **Pro: $59/month.** Unlimited forms, 5 projects and 5 seats (extra projects $10/month each, extra seats $5/month each), team access, rich snippets, and automatic translation of testimonials into 20 languages.
- **Business: $179/month founding offer (listed at $249).** Adds a customer success CRM, outreach campaigns, a proof strategy audit, done-for-you setup, and 1:1 support. Arranged through Senja's support team.

Refunds are handled case by case, according to the pricing FAQ.

## Honest verdict

Senja covers the whole testimonial job, from asking for reviews to putting them on your site, and its AI features save real time because they work from your customers' own words. Product Hunt and G2 reviewers mostly praise how easy it is to set up and how many display options it has. The free plan is a trial in practice, though, and a minority of users report slow embeds and slow support replies.

### Pros

- Easy to set up. Ease of use is the most common praise on Product Hunt and G2.
- Imports from dozens of review and social sites, so your existing proof is usable on day one.
- The AI analysis, case study drafts, and Proof Reels work from real testimonials instead of made-up copy.
- Generous free features (unlimited widgets, analysis, case studies, exports) before you pay anything.
- Monthly plans you can cancel anytime, with exports on every plan.

### Cons

- The free plan hides new testimonials after 15, so anyone collecting seriously ends up on Starter at $29/month.
- Starter allows only 1 project and 2 seats, which pushes agencies and multi-brand businesses to Pro.
- Some users on Reddit and Product Hunt say Senja's embeds slowed their pages down. Check your page speed after adding a widget.
- Support gets mixed reports. Several reviewers praise the founders' help, but others describe unanswered emails. On Senja's feedback board, the founder said in October 2025 that a new engineer had joined to speed up support.
- Rich snippets are Pro-only, and Google does not show review stars for most testimonials a business publishes about itself, as one independent review points out.
