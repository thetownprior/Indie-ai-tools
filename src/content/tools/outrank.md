## What it is

Outrank is an AI SEO platform that runs a company blog for you. You connect your site and it researches keywords you could rank for (with search volume and difficulty), builds a content plan, and writes one SEO article a day, with source links, AI images, and relevant YouTube videos in the article. It then publishes each article on a schedule to WordPress, WordPress.com, Webflow, Shopify, Wix, Framer, Notion, Ghost, a Next.js blog, or any system through a custom webhook. Articles can go out as live posts or as drafts.

It does more than write. Automated backlink building places links to your site inside articles that Outrank writes for its other customers. An Improvements feature reads your Google Search Console data, finds pages that are slipping or ranking just short of the top results, and drafts a rewrite for you to approve. Paid add-ons cover more articles, more backlinks, human editing, and small free tools that it builds and puts on your site. There is also a REST API, an MCP server, and a command-line tool, so AI coding agents can manage keywords, articles, and posts.

Outrank was founded in 2024 by Tibo and Eugene Zolotarenko and is run by Outrank SAS, a company in Paris. It ranked #1 on its Product Hunt launch day. Its own about page lists 4,689 customer websites with Search Console connected as of September 25, 2026.

## Who it's for

Outrank fits solo founders, indie SaaS makers, Shopify stores, and small agencies that know they should be blogging for search but have no writer and no time. It works best for someone who can spend some time each week reviewing drafts and deleting posts that miss the mark, rather than someone who wants to switch it on and walk away.

It is a poor fit for established brands with a reputation to protect, for sites in "your money or your life" topics such as health, finance, or legal advice, and for anyone who wants to approve every outbound link on their site before it goes live.

## Standout details

- **One plan, everything included.** The $99/month plan covers 30 articles, unlimited keyword research, backlink building, AI images, unlimited users, and every publishing integration. There are no per-seat charges.
- **Publishes for you.** Native integrations with ten platforms plus a webhook mean posts land on your own site on the days you pick, as drafts or live.
- **Backlinks on a credit system.** You earn credits by letting Outrank place links to other customers inside your own articles, and spend them on links to your site. Outrank says it checks each placement and re-checks that links stay live.
- **Refreshes old posts.** Improvements uses Search Console data to rewrite pages that are slipping or close to the top results. By default each rewrite waits for your approval, and you can compare changes section by section.
- **150+ languages.** Articles can be generated in more than 150 languages.
- **Free blog hosting.** Outrank Blogs hosts a blog on your own domain or a subfolder such as yourbrand.com/blog, free up to 10,000 visits a month per blog, even without a subscription.
- **Agent-friendly.** A REST API, an MCP server, and an npm command-line tool (outrank-cli) let AI agents create, schedule, and update posts.

## Pricing

Outrank is paid, with a 3-day free trial. Trial articles carry an Outrank watermark. Prices below are from the [pricing page](https://www.outrank.so/pricing) as of November 16, 2026.

- **Organic Growth plan: $99/month, or $999/year.** 30 articles a month, unlimited keyword research, automated backlink building, AI images, unlimited users, and all integrations.
- **More articles for one site:** 60 a month for +$85/month, or 90 a month for +$160/month.
- **Backlink plans:** Premium $199/month, Scale $899/month, Enterprise $1,899/month, for extra monthly backlink credits.
- **Other add-ons:** Free Tools Builder $99/month, Premium Images $49/month, Human Curated Service (editors review and fact-check every article) $1,399/month, and a one-time Directory Submission Service for $500.
- **Volume discounts:** 10% off for 2 to 4 sites, 15% for 5 to 19, and 20% for 20 to 25. Over 25 sites is custom pricing.

Outrank's terms allow first-time subscribers a full refund if they ask by email within 7 calendar days of their first payment. After that, all sales are final.

## Honest verdict

Outrank is a quick, cheap way to put a steady stream of search-targeted posts on your own site, and some solo founders report real results. One Reddit user credited it with their first paying customer from organic search after two months. The same reviewer said more than half the posts it wrote were off-topic for their product, though. Others on Reddit say the content didn't rank and the backlinks came from low-relevance sites. Treat it as a drafting and publishing machine that needs an editor, not an autopilot.

### Pros

- One clear price for 30 published articles a month, with unlimited users and no per-seat fees.
- Keyword research, writing, images, and publishing are all handled, and setup is easy, according to reviewers on Product Hunt, Trustpilot, and Reddit.
- Wide publishing support, including Shopify, Webflow, Framer, Ghost, and a webhook for anything else.
- Improvements rewrites slipping pages and waits for your approval by default.
- The free blog hosting and the API, MCP server, and CLI are useful extras for technical founders.

### Cons

- Article quality and relevance get mixed reports. Several Reddit reviewers say many posts were off-topic or read like obvious AI text, so you need to review and prune them.
- The backlink exchange is the biggest risk. You earn link credits by letting Outrank place other customers' links in your own articles, and its terms say these links are created and published automatically, with no manual preview or approval step. Google's spam policies list "excessive link exchanges" and links made by "automated programs or services" as link spam. Outrank's terms put the risk of any search penalty on you.
- Google's spam policies also name "scaled content abuse", meaning many AI pages made without adding value for users. Publishing 30 posts a month without editing them puts you close to what that policy describes.
- Billing and cancellation complaints keep coming up. Multiple Trustpilot reviewers describe being charged after trying to cancel a trial or not finding a cancel option, plus slow or missing support replies. Learn how to cancel before your 3-day trial ends.
- Refunds are only for first-time subscribers within 7 days. After that, payments are final.
- Results take months. Outrank's own results data reports a median of 2.0× Google clicks after about 7 months, not weeks, and the extra backlink and human-editing add-ons quickly cost more than the base plan.
